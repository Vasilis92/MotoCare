MVP 1 - Local Solo Rider

Add a bike profile (name, model, odometer).

Track key parts: oil, chain, tires, clutch.

Log maintenance events (date, odometer, action, notes).

Show simple reminder status: OK / Due Soon / Overdue based on odometer.

Data stored locally (SQLite or even JSON file).

Export/import data as CSV.


MVP 2 - Connected Rider

Installable PWA (works on mobile & desktop).

Sync data with Google Sheets (backup & view outside app).

Local notifications: "Oil due soon" or "Chain clean overdue."

Dashboard: one-glance view of all parts + next service km.


MVP 3 - Community Rider

Multi-bike support (switch between bikes).

Share/export service history for selling bike or insurance.

Cloud sync option (Supabase/Firebase) instead of only Sheets.

Custom parts (beyond oil/chain/tires/clutch).

Reminders by time as well as mileage (e.g., tire check every 6 months).
